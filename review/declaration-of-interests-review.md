## Disclosure policy

- Update the page whenever a material role begins or ends and review it at least annually.
- Put the last-updated date near the top.
- Treat the standing page as context, not a replacement for article-specific disclosures.
- For each new paper, declare relationships relevant to its topic, data, setting, intervention, or interpretation even if they already appear on the standing page.
- Distinguish direct funding from in-kind operational support, data or facility access, and ordinary salary or contracted time.
- Name former relationships when they remain relevant and use dates where they are known.
- Do not imply that an organisation endorses James's views unless it has explicitly done so.
- Record substantive changes in Git and consider a short public change history if the page grows materially.
